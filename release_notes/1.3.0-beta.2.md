## New

- **Hold Guide to open the stream menu.** Holding a controller's Guide button for the chosen time opens the stream menu without sending Guide to the PC; let go earlier and Guide goes out as a short tap. Pressing another button or a D-pad direction while Guide waits sends Guide first, so Steam chords such as Guide+RB keep working. The new setting offers Off, 0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2 s, and is Off by default. Based on work from RamazanKara/nightjar.
- **Faster video reception.** Video UDP packets are now received in batches, which lowers the CPU cost of receiving the stream.
- **Controllers announced when the stream starts.** Every attached gamepad is reported to the host as soon as the connection starts, instead of on its first input, and gamepads plugged in mid-stream are announced right away. Touchpads, motion sensor nodes and remotes are skipped. Based on work from bradloh/moonlight-android.

## Fixes

- **Faster video recovery.** After 8 lost frames in a row the stream asks the host for a new keyframe, instead of waiting for 120.
